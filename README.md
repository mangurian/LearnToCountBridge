LearnToCount is an instructional aid to help contract / duplicate bridge players learn to keep track
of whcich players have played which cards and how many high card points each has played.
        instructions 
            1. Starting a Hand: When you open the app or click 'Start New Hand', 
            you will be prompted to select who is making the Opening Lead (N, E, S, or W).
			
            2. Playing Cards: Click on a card in the 52-card deck at the top to play it. 
            It will automatically jump to the table in front of the active player.
			
            3. Game Flow: Play proceeds clockwise. After 4 cards are played, the program 
            determines who won the trick based on the Trump Suit (set at the bottom). 
            The winner of the trick automatically leads the next trick.
			
            4. Hand History: As cards are played, they cascade cleanly in front of each 
            player on the table. You can visually track the entire history of the hand.
			
            5. HCP Tracking: The High Card Points (A=4, K=3, Q=2, J=1) played by each player 
            are calculated automatically and displayed under their name.
			
            6. Undo: Made a mistake? Click 'Undo Last Click' at any time to step backwards.
			
			This application should not be used during competition. It is solely designed to facilitate learning to count on practice hands.
